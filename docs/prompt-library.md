# Prompt Library

Prompts in this file are placeholders for future human-reviewed AI workflows. Do not enable autonomous sending from these prompts without review, logging, and approval controls.

## Assessment Insight Summary

Purpose: Turn assessment answers into a concise internal summary.

Inputs:

- Person role.
- Company profile.
- Assessment answers.
- Operational maturity score.
- Known CRM history.

Output:

- Likely operational pain.
- Maturity gap summary.
- Recommended discovery angle.
- Confidence level.
- Missing information.

## Personalized Outreach Draft

Purpose: Draft a first-touch message grounded in the assessment.

Guidelines:

- Be specific to the stated operational gap.
- Avoid hype, pressure, and generic automation language.
- Sound like a useful advisor, not a mass outbound sequence.
- Keep the message short enough for a busy operator.
- Include one clear invitation to continue the conversation.

## CRM Follow-Up Note

Purpose: Create a short CRM note after assessment processing.

Output:

- Score and maturity band.
- Top two apparent constraints.
- Suggested next step.
- Any missing fields that should be researched.

## Enrichment Research Brief

Purpose: Ask an enrichment agent to gather context before outreach.

Output:

- Company business model.
- Recent growth or operational signals.
- Likely decision makers.
- Technology or process clues.
- Sources used.
